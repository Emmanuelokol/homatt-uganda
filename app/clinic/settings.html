local_hospital Clinic Basic Information
qr_code_2 Clinicians

A clinician holds their own Homatt account. Show them this code and they can work here — recording treatments under their own name — for as long as you allow. They never see your money, your stock counts or your reports, and you can stop their access at any moment.

badge Existing logins

Logins already created for this clinic, and what each one can reach. Owners access everything — finances, stock, settings. Clinicians and nurses get treatments, patient history, bookings, quick sale and stock — but not financial reports. Receptionists handle the front desk. Sales / Drug shop attendants get only quick sale and stock.

mic The floating microphone

A microphone button that follows you around the app. Tap it anywhere, say who the patient is and what they came with, and it opens the treatment already filled in. It sits on top of the screen, so how see-through it is matters — set it here, or turn it off.

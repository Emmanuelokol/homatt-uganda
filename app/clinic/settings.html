Create logins for your team and choose what each person can see. Owners access everything — finances, stock, settings. Clinicians and nurses get consultations, patient history, bookings, quick sale and stock — but not financial reports. Receptionists handle the front desk — bookings, history, payments and quick sales. Sales / Drug shop attendants get only quick sale and stock — ideal for a drug shop.
